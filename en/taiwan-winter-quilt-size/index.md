## Check Your Bed Size First, Then Choose the Quilt Size

A quilt should be larger than the bed so it covers both sides and the foot. Like beds, quilts in Taiwan stores are sized in “尺”: the first number is the width and the second is the length. Below we use the common conversion of 1 尺 ≈ 30.3 cm (we did not find an official Taiwan document for this conversion). ## Weight, Filling, and Whether It Can Be Washed

Quilts all called “winter quilts” (冬被) can differ a lot in thickness and material. Before you order, check these three things:

- **Weight** — Stores usually list the weight in kg to show how thick a quilt is. The seller of our pick lists the double at 3.2 kg, which makes it a fairly heavy quilt; if you prefer something light, choose a lower weight. We did not test how warm it is.
- **Filling** — Common fillings are polyester, cotton, wool, down, and silk; go by the fiber content on the label. The BSMI lists wording such as “超細纖維” (microfiber) as an example of an incorrect fiber name, so if you see a name like this, it is worth asking what the actual fiber is.
- **Washability** — Check the care label and the product description. In the same store, the Tencel quilt option is marked “do not wash or tumble dry”, while our pick is listed as washable in water and tumble-dryable. The heavier the quilt, the bigger the washing machine it needs; if your dorm machine is small, you can use a large machine at a self-service laundromat (自助洗衣店).

## When It Arrives, Check the Label: What the Official Rules Require

According to documents from the Bureau of Standards, Metrology and Inspection (BSMI/標準檢驗局), quilts are “bedding” (寢具) subject to mandatory inspection (bedding made of down or feathers and custom-made items are excluded). They must pass inspection and carry a commodity inspection mark before they can be sold. The BSMI's sample quilt label includes the following:

- Commodity inspection mark: in the sample, it is the letter “M” followed by numbers, on the product itself or its smallest package. The seller of our pick lists BSMI number M23194; we did not verify it.
- Fiber content: the outer fabric, lining, and filling are listed separately; any fiber making up 5% or more must be listed by name and percentage by weight.
- Size, country of origin, and the name, phone number, and address of the manufacturer or importer.
- Care label: symbols showing whether it can be washed, bleached, dried, and ironed.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What size quilt fits a 5 尺 double bed in Taiwan?**
The stores we saw usually pair it with a 6×7 尺 double quilt, about 182×212 cm. That is about 30 cm wider and 24 cm longer than a 5 尺 bed (about 152×188 cm), so it covers both sides and the foot. This is a common store practice; we did not find an official rule. The seller of our pick lists the double at 180×210 cm.

**Can I use a double quilt on a 3 尺 or 3.5 尺 single bed?**
Yes, but the quilt will hang down to the floor, and it is heavier and harder to wash. Our pick weighs 3.2 kg in the double size and 2.3 kg in the single size; if your room is small or you wash it yourself, a single quilt is easier.

**What if the quilt is too heavy for the dorm washing machine?**
The seller of our pick recommends a washing machine of 20 kg or more and low-heat drying below 60°C. If your dorm machine is small, you can use a large machine at a self-service laundromat; check the care label before washing. The seller also recommends using a duvet cover, which can be washed more often.

**Do down quilts also have a commodity inspection mark?**
Under the BSMI's scope for bedding subject to inspection, bedding made of down or feathers is excluded, so a down quilt may not have an inspection mark. Quilts filled with polyester, cotton, and similar materials are included and must carry the mark. Whether a specific product is covered depends on the BSMI's announcements.

**What is the CLO warmth rating the seller lists?**
CLO is a unit of measurement for the thermal insulation of clothing. The seller of our pick lists CLO 4.5, but we did not find how this number was measured, so figures from different sellers may not be directly comparable.
