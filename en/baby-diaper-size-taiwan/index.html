Baby Diaper Sizes in Taiwan: NB, S, M, L, XL by Weight, and What to Do When Your Baby Fits Two Sizes (紙尿褲)

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Baby diapers are sized by letter, but one weight often falls inside the range of two sizes, so it is hard to know which pack to buy. This page first explains how sizes line up with weight, then summarizes the seller's listing for one Shopee-direct diaper we found.

When Your Baby's Weight Fits Two Sizes

By this listing, a 5 kg baby fits NB or S, 7 kg fits S or M, 10 kg fits M or L, and 13 kg fits L or XL. We did not find an official rule for choosing inside an overlap. The points below are general checks we put together, not an official standard.

  • Read the pack — Weight ranges and advice differ by brand. Go by the packaging and instructions on the diaper you are holding.
  • Look at your baby's build, not only weight — Two babies of the same weight can have different waist and thigh measurements. After putting one on, check whether the waist, belly, and upper thighs fit and whether the tabs fasten well.
  • Watch after a try — Look for side leaks and for clear red marks on the skin, then decide whether the next pack should be a different size.
  • Ask a doctor about skin — If your baby's bottom is red, has a rash, or has broken skin, ask a pediatrician. The hospital handout we read only covers diaper rash care, not diaper sizes.
