# How to Choose Cockroach Bait (蟑螂餌劑) in Taiwan: Bait Stations, Gel Bait, and Sprays, and the Cost per Station

Last updated: October 24, 2026
選品索引 ChoiceIndex — https://choiceindex.tw/en/

In a rental, a dorm, a kitchen, or a bathroom, seeing one cockroach usually means there are more you cannot see. The common ways to deal with them are bait stations, gel bait, and sprays, and how they work, where to put them, and the risk to children and pets all differ. This guide explains the 3 types, 4 things to check, and how to work out the cost of each station, so you can decide which one to buy.

## First, Understand the 3 Common Types of Cockroach Control

| Type | Features | Who it is for |
|---|---|---|
| Bait station (bait box) | A small box holds a poisoned bait and is placed along the paths cockroaches use. They go in, eat the bait, and carry the poison back to the nest. The bait is enclosed in a casing, so it is not directly exposed. Once placed, the station needs little looking after, and it usually states how long it keeps working, after which you replace it | Renters and ordinary households who want to leave it in place for a long time and not deal with it every day |
| Gel bait (squeeze tube) | A gel in a tube that you put directly into cracks, corners, and the inside of cupboards. You can place it very flexibly, including narrow gaps a bait station cannot fit into, but the gel is exposed, so take special care about children and pets touching it | People with a lot of cockroaches who need precise placement |
| Spray / insecticide | Knocks down a cockroach you can see quickly after contact, so you notice the effect right away, but the effect is short and it usually cannot deal with cockroaches hiding in cracks. After spraying, keep the room ventilated and watch food and tableware | People who want to deal with the one in front of them right now |

No single type is "the best". It mainly depends on whether you want long-term prevention or an immediate fix, whether there are children or pets at home, and how many cockroaches there are. In most cases a bait station or gel bait is the long-term line of defense, and a spray is only for the one you see right now. Also, if you use a spray and bait in the same area, read the pack first, because an insecticide spray can stop cockroaches from going to the bait.

## 4 Things to Check When Choosing Cockroach Bait

| What to check | What to look at |
|---|---|
| Legal labeling (environmental pesticide permit) | Cockroach bait is an environmental pesticide, so the shop page or pack should show an environmental pesticide permit number and the details of the responsible company in Taiwan. We do not recommend buying a product with no number or an unclear source |
| How long it works, and when to replace | A bait station usually states how long it keeps working, for example "effective for 12 months". This is the maker's figure, and the real time depends on the place, how many cockroaches there are, and where you put it. Replace it when it expires or the bait has been eaten |
| How many in a pack, and the cost of each | Do not look only at the total price. Work out "total price ÷ count" for the cost of each station. The more stations you have, the more places you can cover, since the kitchen, bathroom, behind the fridge, and under the sink each need one |
| Safety and placement limits | Check whether the pack says to keep away from children and pets and not to place it above food or on tableware, and check that where you put it is not too damp or too close to a heat source |

## How to Work Out the Cost per Station So the Count in a Pack Does Not Fool You

Do not look only at "how much for one pack". Work out "total price ÷ count". Suppose one pack of 6 costs $129, which is about $21.5 each. Another pack of 12 costs $230, which is about $19.2 each. The unit price looks close, but you should also take into account the stated working time and whether you need to replace them often. It is more accurate to compare the cost per station per month: a station stated to work for 12 months at about $21.5 is about $21.5 ÷ 12 ≈ $1.8 per month, while a station stated to work for 3 months may work out more expensive. This calculation assumes the maker's stated working time, and the real time differs with the place. Also, bait only solves part of the problem: food scraps, standing water, trash, and gaps in the wall are the root reasons cockroaches appear, and if you do not deal with them, even a more expensive bait station only treats the symptom.

## Choosing by Situation

- **A rental or dorm, where you do not want to deal with it every day** — Look first at bait stations. Once placed you can leave them, and one pack of 6 can be spread across the kitchen, bathroom, balcony entrance, and behind the fridge.
- **Many cockroaches and many gaps** — You can consider adding gel bait for narrow gaps that stations cannot reach, but make sure children and pets cannot touch it.
- **Children or pets at home** — A bait station is safer than gel bait, but it still needs to go where children and pets cannot reach (for example deep inside a cupboard or in a gap under the fridge). Do not feel safe just because it has a shell and leave it in the middle of the floor.
- **You have just moved in and want to prevent them first** — In the first days, put stations in the kitchen, the bathroom, and where cardboard boxes are stacked, and combine this with removing outer cartons and sealing food. This works better than waiting until cockroaches appear.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Where is the best place to put a cockroach bait station?**
Put it along the routes cockroaches walk or hide on: under the kitchen counter, beside the gas stove, behind the fridge, under the sink, in bathroom corners, and at balcony entrances and wall corners. Cockroaches like to move along walls and gaps, so the middle of an open floor is usually less effective. Also keep it where children and pets cannot reach, and do not put it above food or tableware.

**Is it normal to see more cockroaches after putting out bait stations?**
It can be. Bait draws out cockroaches hiding in the nest to feed, so in the first week or two you may briefly see more, which is common and does not mean it is not working. But if you still see many after more than a month, check whether food, water, and gaps have not been dealt with, or whether there are so many that you need other methods or a professional pest controller.

**Can bait and insecticide spray be used together?**
We do not recommend using them together in the same area. The smell and residue of a spray can make cockroaches avoid the bait and lower their chance of eating it. If you must deal with the one in front of you, let the air circulate after spraying, put the bait stations along walls and in gaps, and go by the pack instructions.

**How often should I replace bait stations?**
Go by the working time stated on the pack. For example, for a product that says it is effective for 12 months, we suggest replacing it within that time. If the bait has been eaten, the box has got wet, or it is covered in dust or grease, replace it earlier even if it has not expired.
