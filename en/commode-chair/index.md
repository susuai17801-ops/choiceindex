## Buying for an Older Relative: Who to Ask First, and How Subsidies Work

- **Ask a care manager or a long-term care center first** — We do not judge which commode chair suits whom, and we do not cover usage. For which type to buy and whether a subsidy applies, ask a care manager (照管專員), a long-term care center (長照中心), or an assistive device assessor.
- **What we found in the official source** — The attachment to the Ministry of Health and Welfare (衛福部) letter of December 30, 2025, “Assistive Device Assessment Form (format no. 4)”: a commode chair (general type) is item 168 under the subsidy for people with disabilities and EA01-2 under long-term care assistive devices; the form states that the recommended device may be purchased only after approval by the competent authority; a chair without wheels must have adjustable seat height, and a wheeled one must have a pelvic belt; the document also has a checkbox recommending booking a free fitting service at an assistive device center after getting the device. The document does not state subsidy amounts, and neither do we.
- **Assessed and approved first, then buy** — As that document is written, applying for a subsidy means going through assessment and approval by the competent authority before purchase. We did not find whether buying on an online marketplace first and applying afterward is allowed. Ask before ordering.
- **We do not cover usage** — This page only sorts out buying directions; it is not care or medical advice.

## Before You Order, Check These

- Ask a care manager or long-term care center: is there a subsidy you want to apply for, and does it need assessment and approval first?
- Measure the spot for the chair and the doorway width, and the user's seated height, then ask the seller for the height range.
- Read the options: what is the difference between “標配款”, “升級款”, and “聯動調節馬桶扶手”; if unsure, ask the seller.
- Check whether the bucket and cushion are included (the description says “送便桶、坐墊”) and the dimensions the seller lists.
- Save screenshots of the listing page (specs, weight rating, return terms).
