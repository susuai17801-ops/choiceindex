## What the Official Rules Say

- **Motorcycle riders and passengers must wear helmets** — Article 88 of the Road Traffic Safety Rules (道路交通安全規則) on the national laws and regulations database (we read the original text on November 1, 2026) says: the driver and the passenger behind must both wear helmets. It also says small light motorcycles must not carry a passenger, while heavy and ordinary light motorcycles with a fixed seat behind the driver may carry one person; a passenger must not sit sideways.
- **What the helmet must meet** — Paragraph two of the same article (for riders and passengers of motorcycles other than cabin motorcycles) says: 1) the helmet must be one made for riding a motorcycle, passed by the Bureau of Standards, Metrology and Inspection (標檢局), and carry the product inspection mark on the shell; 2) the shell and its parts must be complete, with nothing damaged, loose, or altered; 3) when worn, the helmet must face forward and sit in the correct position, the buckle must be fastened under the chin, the helmet must fit the head shape and sit firmly without shifting up, down, left, or right, and it must not block the view.
- **Penalties** — Article 31 of the Act Governing the Punishment of Violations of Road Traffic Regulations (道路交通管理處罰條例) (we read the original text on November 1, 2026) says: a motorcycle that carries people or goods not as required is fined NT$300 to NT$600 against the driver; a driver or passenger not wearing a helmet as required is fined NT$500 against the driver.
- **Age rules** — The two articles we read do not give an age threshold, and do not give child-specific helmet rules. Whether other rules exist on carrying children, we did not check one by one, so this page does not say “below what age a child can or cannot ride”; follow the authorities' announcements.
- **What we did not read** — We did not read the full text of CNS 2396 or any Bureau explanation specific to kids' helmets; we also did not check the seller's R63555 in the Bureau's system.
- **We do not cover use** — This page only sorts out buying directions and is not safety or legal advice; for use see the seller's notes, and for the rules, follow the authorities' announcements.
