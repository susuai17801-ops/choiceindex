## What the Official Rules Say

- **Riders who hold a phone can be fined** — Article 31-1 of the Act Governing the Punishment of Violations of Road Traffic Regulations (道路交通管理處罰條例) on the national laws and regulations database (we read the original text on November 2, 2026), paragraph 2, says: a motorcycle driver who, while on the road, uses a mobile phone, computer, or similar device by hand to dial, talk, communicate data, or do other things that affect driving safety is fined NT$1,200. Paragraph 1 covers the same conduct by car drivers, with a fine of NT$3,000.
- **The article says “by hand”** — The element in the article is “by hand” (以手持方式), and it does not mention phone holders. Whether operating a phone that sits in a holder counts as a violation, we have only seen news reports and have not read the original interpretation letter, so we do not judge it; follow the authorities' announcements and the police's determination.
- **What we did not read** — We did not read the implementing and publicity rules of Article 31-1 or any interpretation letter about phone holders; the damping and anti-theft effects the seller lists have not been tested by us either.
- **We do not cover how to use it** — This page only organizes buying directions and is not safety or legal advice; for use, see the product manual and the seller's notes, and for the rules follow the authorities' announcements.
