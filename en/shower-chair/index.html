How to Choose a Shower Chair for an Older Adult in Taiwan (洗澡椅): Backrest and Armrests, Seat Height, Size, Weight Rating, and Subsidies Need Assessment First

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Searching “洗澡椅” on Shopee Taiwan returns prices from a few hundred to over a thousand NT dollars. Product titles often mention older adults, pregnant women, shower-and-toilet chairs, and anti-slip bathroom chairs all at once, making it difficult to distinguish between listings. We checked official Taiwanese sources: the “Assistive Device Assessment Form” attached to a Ministry of Health and Welfare letter dated December 30, 2025 lists the shower chair as both a subsidized assistive device for people with disabilities (item 167) and a long-term care assistive device (EA01-3), and states that the recommended device may be purchased only after the competent authority approves it; the spec requirements for the subsidized type include a backrest, and a seat height adjustment for models without wheels. We did not find an official buying guide or weight standard for shower chairs, and we did not find a mandatory inspection rule for adult shower chairs. Buying for an Older Adult: Whom to Ask First, and How to Read the Subsidy

  • Ask the long-term care case manager (照管專員) or the local long-term care center first — We do not judge which shower chair suits whom, and we do not discuss how to use it. For which type to buy and whether there is a subsidy, ask a long-term care case manager, a long-term care center (長照中心), or an assistive device assessor.
  • What we found in official sources — The attachment to the Ministry of Health and Welfare letter dated December 30, 2025, No. 衛部顧字第1141963386B號, “Assistive Device Assessment Form (format no. 4)”: the shower chair is item 167 under the assistive device subsidy for people with disabilities and EA01-3 under long-term care assistive devices, and the two cannot both be subsidized within the minimum service life; the form states the recommended device may be purchased only after the competent authority approves it; the subsidized type must have a backrest, models without wheels need a seat height adjustment, and wheeled models must come with a pelvic belt. This document does not state the subsidy amount, and we do not state one either.
  • Assessment and approval first, then buy — According to the document above, to apply for a subsidy the assessment and approval must come before the purchase. We could not find information on whether you can purchase the chair from an online store yourself and apply for the subsidy afterward; confirm this before ordering.
  • We do not cover how to use it — This page only summarizes buying directions and is not care or medical advice.

Before You Order, Confirm These

  • Ask the long-term care case manager or long-term care center first: is there a subsidy you want to apply for? Does it need assessment and approval first?
  • Measure the space in the bathroom and the user's seated height, and check whether the 40–53 cm seat height range is enough.
  • Read the variants carefully: “no seat cushion” or “with seat cushion”; if unsure, ask the seller.
  • The seller asks for video while unboxing and does not accept returns for damage caused by human factors; record before you open.
  • Save a screenshot of the listing (specs, weight rating, and return rules).
