What the Official Rules Say

  • High chairs for children are subject to mandatory inspection — The Bureau of Standards, Metrology and Inspection (標檢局)'s table of items subject to inspection for children's high chairs (應施檢驗兒童用高腳椅檢驗品目明細表, prepared October 25, 2023) lists children's high chairs as subject to mandatory inspection, with the scope written as limited to high chairs for children aged 6 to 36 months. The Bureau's briefing (October 28, 2025) gave the rules in force at that time: full inspection under CNS 15017 (2013 edition), by surveillance inspection or certification registration.
  • The inspection rules have been amended — The Bureau announced an amendment on March 11, 2026 under No. 經標檢政字第11530003220號 (we read a reposted copy of the announcement, not the attachment): the edition of the inspection standard was updated, the inspection method changed to “type-approval batch inspection” or “certification registration”, and the designated code on the product inspection mark must include a QR code; the announcement says it takes effect immediately. The October 2025 briefing, on the other hand, previewed the new standard as CNS 15017 (2018 edition) plus the plasticizer item of CNS 15503, with the implementation date written as “planned January 1, 2027”. We did not read the attachment, so we cannot tell whether there is a transition period; please go by the Bureau's official announcement.
  • Scope and inspection marks — The briefing describes the scope as freestanding high chairs for feeding or meals that raise a child to dining table height, for children under 3 who can sit unaided; multifunction products must meet the relevant standards; table-mounted hook-on chairs and boosters that sit on a chair fall under other standards and are outside the high chair scope. The briefing also says that after the amendment, businesses print the inspection mark themselves, with the letter “T” for type approval and “R” for certification registration, followed by a designated code. The number this seller lists starts with M; we did not check which system it represents, and we do not judge whether this product falls within the scope.
  • We do not cover installation or use — This page only sorts out buying directions; it is not safety or medical advice. For installation and use, see the product manual.
