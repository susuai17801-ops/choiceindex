It does not push any single brand.

## First, When Should You Replace the Filter?

An air purifier's filter collects dust and hair over time, and its filtering efficiency and airflow both drop. Most models show a "remaining filter life" percentage on the panel. This number is more accurate than trying to remember "replace it every so many months". In places with heavy air pollution, in homes with pets, or if the purifier runs almost all day, the filter wears out much faster than in an ordinary home.

## Comparing the 3 Versions

| Version | Filter grade | Antiviral / antibacterial | Formaldehyde removal | Who it is for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Antibacterial (purple) | H13 medical grade | Yes | No | Homes with allergies, asthma, young children, or pets, where limiting germs matters |
| Formaldehyde-removal (blue) | H13 medical grade | No | Yes (a separate formaldehyde-removal inner cylinder) | New renovation or new furniture, where formaldehyde and volatile organic compounds matter |
| Economy (green) | H13 medical grade | No | No | People who simply want basic filtering and dust removal, and put budget first |

The filter grade is usually the same in all three versions, and the difference is in the extra functions. Original filters are mostly H11 grade (basic antibacterial). If a third-party filter says H13 medical grade, it means it goes a step further in filtering fine particles.

## With or Without an RFID Chip: What Is the Difference?

- With an RFID chip: the purifier recognizes the filter correctly once installed, the panel shows the remaining filter life correctly, and it does not show a "please use an original filter" message.
- Without an RFID chip: the filtering itself is not affected, but the purifier cannot recognize the filter automatically, so the panel may show a message, and the filter life does not reset and start counting down by itself. You have to note down the replacement date yourself.

The same style is usually sold in both a with-RFID and a without-RFID version, with the with-RFID one a little more expensive. If you care about the panel message or do not want to track the replacement date yourself, the with-RFID version is less trouble.

## Choosing by Situation

- **Allergic children, asthma, or pets at home** — Look first at the antibacterial version, which strengthens germ control and filtering of fine particles.
- **Just finished a renovation, bought new furniture, or you care about the formaldehyde smell** — Choose the formaldehyde-removal version, which has an extra formaldehyde-removal inner cylinder.
- **You just want to keep the machine working at a basic level with regular replacement** — The economy version is enough. Its filter grade is H13, the same as the other versions.
- **You do not want to keep seeing messages on the machine, and want to track filter life accurately** — Choose a version with an RFID chip.
- **You need to buy several sets at once to stock up** — Check the shop's delivery limits (some shops allow only 2 sets per convenience-store order, and more than that needs home delivery) before you order.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**About how often should I replace the filter in a Xiaomi air purifier?**
Original filters are mostly labeled for about 6 to 12 months, but the real life depends a lot on air quality, hours of use, and room size. In places with heavy air pollution, or if it runs all day, you may need to replace it within a few months. It is best to go by the remaining filter percentage shown on the panel, and not to memorize a number of months.

**What happens if I install a third-party filter without an RFID chip?**
The filtering itself is not affected, but the panel may show a "please use an original filter" message, and the remaining filter life does not reset and count again by itself, so you have to note down the replacement date yourself. A third-party filter with an RFID chip shows the life normally and does not show the message.

**Will a third-party filter damage the machine or affect the warranty?**
The filter itself is a consumable, and replacing it with a third-party one generally does not affect the warranty on the purifier's hardware. But if the purifier itself breaks, the maker may ask when assessing it whether you used third-party consumables. When you buy, the filter grade (such as H13 medical-grade HEPA) and whether the maker has third-party testing (such as SGS) matter more than comparing prices alone.
