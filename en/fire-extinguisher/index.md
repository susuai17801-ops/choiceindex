## For Renters: Whom to Ask Before You Buy

- **Ask the landlord or building management whether an extinguisher is already there** — We did not find an official source saying whether a tenant must supply their own extinguisher, so we cannot say “you must buy one” or “you need not”. Before renting, ask the landlord and building management whether one is already installed and whether there is a maintenance record.
- **The Fire Services Act rule is on the sales side** — Article 12 says fire equipment that has been announced as requiring approval may not be sold, displayed, or installed unless a registered body has approved it and an approval mark has been attached (current text in the national laws database). The article is about products needing approval, not about how many extinguishers a home must have.
- **Registered bodies and two-stage approval** — The article says approval is carried out in order: type approval and then individual approval. The Control Yuan's 2022 corrective action text says the registered bodies issuing approval marks for extinguishers are the Fire Safety Center Foundation and the Republic of China Fire Technology Consultant Foundation. That text is from 2022, and we did not recheck whether the division of work is still the same.
- **We do not teach firefighting or judge safety** — In a fire, follow the National Fire Agency and your local fire department, and call 119. This page only summarizes buying directions and is not safety advice.

## Before You Order, Confirm These

- Check whether the product page shows an approval mark or type approval number; what the spec field says is filled in by the seller, so also check the mark on the unit when it arrives.
- Decide on the 5, 10, or 20 type: measure the spot and think about whether you can lift it.
- Ask where the factory manufacture date sticker is; when it arrives, find the factory sticker first, not a date the seller stuck on.
- Extinguishers cannot ship with other items; for remote islands or distant areas ask about shipping by chat first, and confirm first if you need a paper invoice.
- Read the warranty scope and return rules, and save a screenshot of the listing.
