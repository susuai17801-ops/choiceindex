## What the Official Rules Say

- **Rules for carrying young children in small vehicles** — Under the Ministry of Transportation and Communications' “Regulations on the Implementation and Promotion of Safe Seating for Children in Small Vehicles” (小型車附載幼童安全乘坐實施及宣導辦法; amended March 2, 2020; the amended provisions took effect September 1, 2020), Article 2 defines a “young child” as a child aged 4 or under weighing 18 kg or under, and a “safety seat” as the portable infant bed and child seat described in CNS 11497 (child restraints for motor vehicles). The regulations are issued under Article 31, paragraph 3 of the Act Governing the Punishment of Violation of Road Traffic Regulations; we did not re-check the penalty text, so we do not state a fine here.
- **Age 2 and under, and over 2 up to 4** — Article 4 says: a child aged 2 or under must be placed in the rear seat in a portable infant bed or a rear-facing child seat, restrained or secured; a child over 2 up to 4 weighing 18 kg or under must sit in a child seat in the rear seat, restrained or secured, and a rear-facing child seat is preferred. Each safety seat is limited to one child, must be firmly connected as set out in the product manual, and must match the height or weight limits the product allows. Article 5 lists exceptions: emergency transport, injury, disability, or health harm from using a safety seat, with a certificate from a medical institution; or a passenger other than the driver nursing the child or giving necessary daily care that cannot be done in the seat.
- **Over 4 up to 12** — Article 4 says: a child over 4 up to 12, or over 18 kg up to 36 kg, must sit in the rear seat and use a seat belt under the seat-belt regulations for drivers and passengers; it does not say whether a booster seat is required.
- **Inspection standard and mark** — We read a Bureau of Standards, Metrology and Inspection (標檢局) document that cites announcement No. 經標三字第10930002090號 (April 28, 2020), which says the inspection standard for child restraints for motor vehicles subject to mandatory inspection is CNS 11497 (2019 edition); we did not read the announcement itself or its effective date. The product inspection mark can be looked up in the Bureau's system (civil.bsmi.gov.tw/bsmi_pqn/). The seller of this product lists R45656; we did not check the lookup result and we do not judge whether this product meets the rules.
- **We do not cover installation and use** — This page only sorts out buying directions and is not safety or legal advice; for installation and use see the product manual, and for the rules, consult the National Regulations Database and the authorities' announcements.
