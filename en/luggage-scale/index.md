- What does your suitcase handle look like? → A strap must fit through it, a hook must fit around it

## Official Sources: Airline Baggage Rules and the Taiwan Weights and Measures Act

- **Your allowance depends on your ticket** — The checked baggage page on China Airlines' (中華航空) official website says that in economy class, the number of checked bags per passenger differs by route and booking class. So even on the same airline, different tickets can carry different allowances; follow your ticket and the airline's notices (we read the official website on November 14, 2026).
- **Per-bag weight and size limits (China Airlines)** — The same page says that on flights operated by China Airlines/Mandarin Airlines, a single checked bag over 32 kg (70 lb) cannot be accepted, and each bag's length, width, and height must add up to 158 cm or less.
- **Carry-on baggage (China Airlines)** — China Airlines' carry-on page says economy passengers may bring 1 carry-on bag and 1 personal item; the carry-on may not exceed 56×36×23 cm or 115 cm in combined length, width and height, and each piece may not exceed 7 kg.
- **Other airlines** — We read only China Airlines' official website. Other airlines (including low-cost carriers) may have different rules on pieces, weight, and buying extra baggage, and we did not check them one by one; see your own airline's official website.
- **Does a luggage scale need verification** — Article 5 of Taiwan's Weights and Measures Act (度量衡法) says the competent authority may designate measuring instruments related to transactions, certification, official testing, environmental protection, public safety, and medical care as legal measuring instruments; Article 14 says the designated agency may verify legal measuring instruments (National Laws and Regulations Database, revision date January 21, 2009). The uses named in the article do not include weighing your own bags at home, but we did not find a specific explanation from the Bureau of Standards, Metrology and Inspection (標準檢驗局) about luggage scales.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How many kg of baggage can I take when flying home from Taiwan?**
There is no single answer; it depends on your airline, route, and fare class. Taking China Airlines as an example, its official website says economy checked-bag pieces differ by route and booking class, a single bag over 32 kg is not accepted on its own flights, and each carry-on piece may not exceed 7 kg. Check your ticket and your airline's official website.

**Will my home scale match the airport scale?**
We did not find data comparing luggage scales with airport scales, and the recommended product's page does not state its accuracy. The airport counter's scale is what counts; if you are close to the limit, leave a little margin.

**Does a luggage scale need metrological verification in Taiwan?**
Article 5 of the Weights and Measures Act lists the uses of legal measuring instruments as transactions, certification, official testing, environmental protection, public safety, and medical care, and does not list personal luggage weighing; but we did not find a specific explanation from the Bureau of Standards, Metrology and Inspection about luggage scales.

**Can I take the luggage scale on the plane?**
The recommended product uses a coin cell. We have not checked whether the scale and its battery may be carried in your carry-on or checked baggage; follow your airline's and the civil aviation authority's rules.

**What if my bag is overweight?**
This page covers only choosing a luggage scale, not how to handle overweight bags. For excess baggage fees and information on purchasing additional baggage allowance, see your airline's official website.
