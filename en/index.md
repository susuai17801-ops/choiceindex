## Frequently asked questions

**Is it safe to shop online on Shopee Taiwan?**
Shopee is one of the largest online shopping platforms in Taiwan and is used by millions of local people every day. Still follow the basic safety rules: check the shop's rating and reviews, and never transfer money outside the Shopee system.

**Can I pay without a Taiwanese credit card?**
Yes. Shopee Taiwan offers cash payment at convenience stores (7-Eleven or FamilyMart) when you pick up your order, so a Taiwanese credit card or bank account is not required.

**Can items be delivered to a dormitory or workplace?**
Yes, as long as the address is complete and clear. The most practical choice is usually to pick up your order at the nearest convenience store, so you don't have to wait for a courier at your dormitory or home.

**Do I need to read Chinese to use Shopee Taiwan?**
Most product names are in Chinese, so searching in your own language usually returns nothing. Copy the Chinese keywords listed in each guide and paste them into the search box. You can also use a phone camera translation feature to check the packaging.
