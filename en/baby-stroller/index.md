|---|---|---|

Choose a type that fits your child's age and weight, how you get around, and your storage space at home.

## What the Official Rules Say

- **Strollers are subject to mandatory inspection** — The Bureau of Standards, Metrology and Inspection's (標檢局) “Commodity Inspection Operation Rules for Baby Strollers” (手推嬰幼兒車商品檢驗作業規定; we read the PDF on the Bureau's website, and did not find the issue date or effective date on it) says: the scope is baby and child strollers; the inspection standards include CNS 12940-1 (2022 edition), CNS 12940-2 (seated strollers for children over 15 kg up to 22 kg, 2022 edition), CNS 4797 on toy safety (2019 edition), and the baby stroller labeling standard.
- **Inspection items** — The listed items include chemical hazards (migration of certain elements, phthalate plasticizers, azo colorants, formaldehyde, flame retardants, thermal hazards), mechanical hazards (protective function, entrapment, moving parts, entanglement, choking and ingestion, suffocation, hazardous edges and protrusions, parking and brake devices, stability, structural integrity), and the durability and adhesion of labels. These are the items the inspection rules list, not the inspection result of any particular product.
- **Traditional Chinese labels and warning** — The rules say: labels follow CNS 12940-1, CNS 12940-2, and the labeling standard, placed on the product body or in the manual, together with the warning “本商品不可放置於機車踏墊使用，亦不可以連結器連接其他手推嬰幼兒車使用。” (this product must not be placed on a scooter's footboard and must not be connected to another stroller with a connector). The text should be mainly in Traditional Chinese; apart from the manual, labels must be in a visible place on the product body as a fixed label that does not wear off easily; the word “警告” (warning) must be at least 5 mm and other text at least 2.5 mm.
- **Inspection mark** — The rules say: the inspection mark for lot-by-lot inspection with type approval uses the letter series “T” plus an assigned code; registration-based certification uses “R” plus an assigned code; both systems run in parallel. The number the seller of this product lists is IC20416182, whose format differs from the above; we did not check the lookup result and we do not judge whether this product meets the rules. The inspection mark can be looked up in the Bureau's system (civil.bsmi.gov.tw/bsmi_pqn/).
- **What we did not read** — We did not read the full text of the baby stroller labeling standard, or the 2023 Ministry of Economic Affairs gazette amendment mentioned in search results, so we do not cite details from them; we also did not check penalty amounts.
- **We do not cover use** — This page only sorts out buying directions and is not safety or legal advice; for use see the product manual, and for the rules, follow the authorities' announcements.
