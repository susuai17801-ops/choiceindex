How to Choose Rain Shoe Covers for a Motorcycle (機車雨鞋套) in Taiwan: Fastening, Height, Size, Material and Seams, Sole

Last updated: November 8, 2026

When you ride in the rain, your shoes usually get wet first. Rain shoe covers come in hook-and-loop, zipper, and silicone styles, with different heights and sizes, which can make choosing one confusing. Six things are a good place to start: style and fastening; height and pairing with a raincoat; size and shoe type; material and seams; sole and storage; and warranty and returns. This page goes through them one by one, then recommends one pair we found: the TOPSON quick-on waterproof rain shoe covers (hook-and-loop style) on Shopee, which the seller says have a three-stage adjustable hook-and-loop strap and a 40 cm height meant to be used with a raincoat, and fit high heels, sneakers, and leather shoes.
