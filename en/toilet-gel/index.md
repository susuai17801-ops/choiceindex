# How to Choose Toilet Cleaner (馬桶清潔劑) in Taiwan: Flower Gel, Cleaning Gel, Tablets, and Strong Cleaners

Toilets develop limescale, urine stains, and odors, and a few days after you scrub them they come back. In the store you can find flower gel, hanging gel, cleaning tablets, and strong cleaners, with claims such as "one press and it smells fresh" and "sticks on and does not fall off", but each type is used differently, cleans differently, and suits different situations. This guide explains the 4 types, 4 things to check, and the points about scent and safe use, so you can decide which one to buy.

## First, Understand the 4 Common Types of Toilet Cleaner

| Type | Features | Who it is for |
|---|---|---|
| Flower gel (stamp type) | You press the gel onto the inside of the toilet with a push bottle to form a small flower shape, and each flush slowly dissolves it and releases the cleaning ingredient and scent. It keeps the toilet smelling fresh day to day without a separate brush, and when the flower gets small you can scrub with the toilet brush using the gel as a cleaner | People who want to keep it fresh day to day with little effort, and care about scent |
| Hanging gel, cleaning gel | Placed on the rim of the toilet or fixed in place, and released slowly as you flush. The effect and how long it lasts depend on the product, and some focus on removing odor while others focus on preventing stains | People who want to put it in once and leave it to work, without doing something every time |
| Tank cleaning tablets | Dropped into the toilet tank and released slowly with each flush. Before use, check whether your tank and its parts can take it, because some types may affect the parts inside the tank or change the color of the water | People sure that their tank suits it and who want continuous automatic cleaning |
| Strong cleaners (liquid, foam, strong acid) | Usually stronger at cleaning, and suited to a toilet with limescale or heavy urine stains, used together with the toilet brush. The smell and corrosiveness are stronger, so wear gloves and keep the room ventilated | People dealing with stubborn dirt or doing a regular deep clean |

No single type is "the best". It depends on how dirty the toilet is now and how you want to use it. For keeping a toilet clean day to day with little effort, flower gel or hanging gel is more convenient. A toilet that already has thick limescale or urine stains usually needs a strong cleaner first and then a gel to maintain it. Always read the product instructions first, because the amount, how often, and what it can be used on differ by product. This site only explains how to choose and is not product safety advice.

## 4 Things to Check When Choosing Toilet Cleaner

| What to check | What to look at |
|---|---|
| Does the type match your toilet's condition? | First look at the toilet's current state. For day-to-day upkeep and a light smell, a gel is enough. With thick limescale or yellow stains, a gel alone is usually not enough, and you need a strong cleaner or scrubbing. A cleaner that is too strong, used on a toilet that only needs upkeep, is not necessarily more effective, and needs more safety care |
| Scent and where you use it | Flower gels often come in scents such as floral, soap, mint, cotton, and lavender. Whether a scent suits you differs from person to person. If someone at home is sensitive to smells, or there are babies or pets, or the room is not well ventilated, choose a light scent or no scent, or buy a small pack to try first |
| Format and how to refill | Some come as a set with a push bottle, and some only sell refill packs. The first time you usually need the set with the push bottle, and buying refill packs afterwards is cheaper. Before you order, check whether you chose the push-bottle set or the refill pack, and check the count, because options differ a lot between shops and listings |
| Brand, origin, and where the label comes from | Some listings state the brand, that it is imported from Japan, and where it is made. Claims on a listing are mostly the seller's description. Before you order, check that the pack clearly shows the brand and product name, and watch whether it is sold through a proper channel. We have not checked these claims one by one |

## What to Remember When Using Toilet Cleaner

- **Do not mix with bleach or other cleaners** — Mixing chlorine bleach with an acidic cleaner can produce harmful gas. Use only one at a time, rinse well after use before switching to another, and keep the room ventilated.
- **Wear gloves, and avoid contact with eyes and skin** — Gels and cleaners may irritate the skin or eyes, so wear gloves. If it gets on you, rinse with plenty of water, and see a doctor if you feel unwell.
- **Keep out of reach of children and pets** — Keep gels, push bottles, and refill packs put away to prevent anyone from swallowing or touching them by mistake. If someone swallows it, follow the instructions on the pack and see a doctor right away.
- **Scent and cleaning results differ from person to person** — How strong it cleans and how long it lasts, as stated on a listing, is mostly the seller's description or users' experience. The real effect depends on the toilet material, water quality, and how often you use it, and there is no guarantee.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can flower gel really get a toilet clean?**
Flower gel is more for keeping the toilet fresh and fragrant day to day. It releases cleaning ingredients slowly as you flush and can reduce how much dirt sticks, but a toilet that already has limescale or heavy urine stains usually also needs a toilet brush or a strong cleaner. The real effect depends on the toilet material, water quality, and how often you use it, so go by the product instructions.

**What is the difference between a push-bottle set and a refill pack, and which should I buy?**
A push-bottle set includes the push bottle and one gel, and it is the one to choose the first time. A refill pack has only the gel and is used with a push bottle you already have, so it is cheaper for restocking. Before you order, check the option on the listing, because options differ a lot between listings, and go by the product instructions.

**Can toilet cleaner be used together with bleach?**
We do not recommend it. Mixing chlorine bleach with an acidic cleaner can produce harmful gas. Use only one cleaner at a time, rinse well after use before switching to another, and keep the room ventilated. For details, see the notes on the product pack.

**What should I be careful about with gel if I have children or pets at home?**
Keep the gel and push bottle out of reach of children and pets, to prevent anyone from swallowing or touching them by mistake. If it gets on your skin or in your eyes, rinse with plenty of water, and see a doctor if you feel unwell. If it is swallowed, act as the pack says and see a doctor as soon as you can. If you are sensitive to smells, try a small amount of a stronger-scented type first.
